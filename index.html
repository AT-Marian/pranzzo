FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do customers need to install an app?

No. Guests simply scan the QR code and access the ordering experience through their phone.

Can Pranzzo be used in hotel rooms?

Yes. Hotels can place QR codes inside rooms so guests can browse, order and pay without calling room service.

Where does the order go?

The order is designed to move into the restaurant or hotel's kitchen and POS workflow.

Can restaurants use it at tables?

Yes. QR codes can be placed at individual tables so guests can order directly.
