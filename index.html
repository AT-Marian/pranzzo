FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do customers need to install an app?

No. Guests can access the ordering experience from their phone after scanning the QR code.

Can Pranzzo be used in hotel rooms?

Yes. A QR can be placed in each room so guests can browse, order and pay without calling room service.

Where does the order go?

The ordering flow is designed to connect with the restaurant or hotel kitchen/POS workflow.

Can restaurants use it at tables?

Yes. Each table can use a QR ordering flow for faster and more convenient service.
