How Pranzzo Works

From scanning to serving, Pranzzo makes the ordering experience simple for both guests and staff.

01

Scan

Guests scan the QR code at their table or inside their hotel room.

02

Browse

Guests instantly browse the digital menu from their own device.

03

Order

Customers select their items and place their order in just a few taps.

04

Prepare

The order reaches the kitchen or POS so staff can begin preparation immediately.

05

Serve

Once ready, the order can be served directly to the table or hotel room.

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting started with Pranzzo.

Do customers need to install an app?

No. Customers simply scan the QR code and access the digital ordering experience through their phone's browser.

Can Pranzzo be used in hotel rooms?

Yes. A QR code can be placed inside hotel rooms so guests can browse the menu and order directly from their room.

Where does the order go?

Orders can be connected to the restaurant or hotel's operational workflow, including the kitchen and POS system.

Can restaurants use it at tables?

Yes. Each table can have its own QR code, allowing guests to order directly from their table.
